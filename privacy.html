Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Stack & Deliver (the "game") on iPhone or iPad. The game is made by MJAJ Games ("we", "us"). In short: we don't collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. The only outside services are Google AdMob, which shows adverts, and Apple, which handles purchases.

1. What's stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only. This includes your Tips, town, missions, outfits, best scores, settings and whether you've bought Remove Ads. We never receive this data. It stays on your device until you use Settings → Reset progress or delete the app.

2. Adverts (Google AdMob)

The game shows adverts using Google AdMob, a service from Google. When an advert loads, the AdMob software in the game may send Google some information so it can deliver the advert, count views and prevent fraud. This can include:

  • your device type, operating system and app version
  • your IP address, which can show your approximate location such as your country or city
  • how you interact with adverts (for example, whether one was shown or tapped)
  • diagnostic information, such as crashes in the advert software

We only ask for non-personalised adverts. Google chooses adverts from general information such as approximate location and the game itself, not from a profile of you. The game doesn't ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so iOS keeps your device's advertising identifier hidden from the game.

Google handles this information under its own policies. To find out more, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Consent (UK, EU and similar regions)

If you're in the UK, the European Economic Area, Switzerland or another region where the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent form before any adverts load. You choose whether Google may use cookies or similar storage on your device for adverts. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. Your choice is stored on your device.

4. Purchases (Apple)

The optional Remove Ads purchase is handled completely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, email, payment card or Apple Account details. The game only learns whether the purchase went through, so it can switch off the between-run adverts. Read Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles payments.

5. Reward videos are optional

Some buttons offer a reward, such as Revive, Double Tips or Free Tips, in exchange for watching a video advert. These are always optional, and the game works fully without them.

6. Children

The game isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone. Advert content is limited to Google's "Parental guidance" rating or below.

7. Your rights

We don't hold personal information about you, so there's nothing for us to give you or delete. You can delete all game data yourself by resetting your progress or deleting the app. To exercise your rights over information Google holds, use Google's tools linked above. If you're in the UK or EU, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. In the UK, that's the Information Commissioner's Office.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
