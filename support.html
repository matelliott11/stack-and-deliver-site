If you're reporting a bug, it helps to tell us your device (for example "iPhone 13"), your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it back on a new device?

Open the game, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

What does Remove Ads remove?

It removes the adverts that sometimes appear between runs. The optional reward videos (Revive, Double Tips and Free Tips) stay, because you only see them when you choose to.

A reward button has disappeared.

Reward buttons only appear when an advert is ready to play. If you're offline, or there's no advert available right now, the button hides. Try again in a little while.

Does the game need the internet?

No. You can play fully offline. You only need a connection for adverts and purchases.

How do I change my advert privacy choices?

If you're in the UK, the EU or another region where the law requires it, go to Settings and tap Privacy choices. If you don't see that button, it isn't needed where you are. Find out more in the privacy policy.

How do I start again from scratch?

Go to Settings and tap Reset progress. This deletes your Tips, town, missions, outfits and best scores, and it can't be undone. It doesn't remove a Remove Ads purchase.

I lost my progress.

Your progress is saved only on your device. There are no accounts or cloud saves. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted too. Purchases can always be restored (see above).
